# ✈️ Flight Delay Analysis

## Analyzing Airline Delays, Trends & Operational Performance

A Power BI data analytics portfolio project focused on analyzing flight delays, airline performance, cancellation rates, delay severity, monthly trends, and airport-level patterns.

The project transforms flight operations data into an interactive dashboard designed to support data-driven understanding of airline operational performance.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Flight delays can affect operational efficiency, passenger experience, and airline performance.

The objective of this project was to analyze flight delay data and identify patterns that could help highlight areas requiring closer operational monitoring and improvement.

The analysis focuses on:

* Delay volume
* Airline performance
* Average delay per flight
* Monthly delay trends
* Delay severity
* Airport-level performance
* Flight cancellations

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project was designed to:

1. Measure the overall scale of flight delays.
2. Identify airlines with higher numbers of delayed flights.
3. Compare average delay performance across airlines.
4. Analyze how delays vary throughout the year.
5. Examine the severity of flight delays.
6. Identify airport-level delay patterns.
7. Provide actionable recommendations based on the analysis.

---

## ❓ Key Business Questions

The dashboard was developed to answer:

* How much delay is occurring?
* How many flights are delayed?
* Which airlines have the highest number of delayed flights?
* Which airlines have the highest average delay per flight?
* When are delays higher or lower?
* Where are delays occurring?
* How severe are the delays?
* What is the cancellation rate?

## 🔄 Methodology

The project followed a structured analytics workflow:

**Data → Preparation → Analysis → Visualization → Insights → Recommendations**

### 1. Data Preparation

The flight operations data was reviewed and prepared for analysis using Power Query.

The dataset contains separate delay fields including:

* Carrier Delay
* Weather Delay
* NAS Delay
* Security Delay
* Late Aircraft Delay

These fields represent different delay categories within the dataset.

### 2. Data Analysis

DAX measures and calculated metrics were used to evaluate:

* Total Delay Minutes
* Average Delay Per Flight
* Delayed Flights
* Cancellation Rate
* Delay Severity

### 3. Visualization

The analysis was presented through an interactive Power BI dashboard containing:

* KPI cards
* Delayed Flights by Airline
* Average Delay Per Flight by Airline
* Average Delay Per Flight by Month
* Distribution of Delay Severity
* Average Delay by Origin Airport

## 🔎 Key Findings

### 1. Significant Delay Volume

The analysis recorded approximately **63.52 million total delay minutes** across approximately **1 million delayed flights**.

### 2. Most Delays Are Minor

**89.24%** of delays fall into the **Minor (<30 minutes)** category, while **5.59%** are classified as Moderate (30–60 minutes).

### 3. Airline Performance Varies

Average delay performance differs across airlines.

Among the airlines displayed in the analysis:

* **EV — 15.52 minutes**
* **OH — 15.24 minutes**
* **B6 — 12.87 minutes**
* **FL — 12.30 minutes**
* **AA — 11.88 minutes**

This shows why both delayed-flight volume and average delay duration are useful measures when evaluating airline performance.

### 4. Delays Vary Throughout the Year

The monthly analysis shows changes in average delay performance across the year, helping identify periods that may require closer operational monitoring.

### 5. Airport-Level Patterns

The airport visualization provides a geographical view of average delay performance and helps identify where delays are occurring.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could support improved operational performance:

### 1. Prioritize High-Delay Airlines

Investigate airlines consistently recording higher average delays and identify potential operational bottlenecks.

### 2. Monitor High-Risk Periods

Use monthly delay trends to anticipate periods of increased delays and support proactive resource planning.

### 3. Investigate Airport-Level Patterns

Identify airports with consistently higher average delays and investigate potential local operational factors.

### 4. Reduce Recurring Minor Delays

Although most delays are minor, their high frequency can accumulate into a substantial amount of lost operational time.

### 5. Establish Continuous KPI Monitoring

Regularly monitor:

* Delay volume
* Average delay
* Delayed flights
* Cancellation rate
* Airline performance

This can help identify emerging performance issues and support data-driven operational decisions.
